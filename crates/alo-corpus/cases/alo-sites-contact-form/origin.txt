## What it found

- **A `gap` written with a function was read as no gap** — item 397. The
  section is `display: grid; grid-template-columns: .8fr 1.2fr; gap:
  clamp(2rem, 6vw, 5rem)`. Layout split the `gap` shorthand at every space,
  so `clamp(2rem,`, `6vw,` and `5rem)` each failed to read and were dropped
  without a record: the columns were 304 and 456, touching, and the
  introduction sat directly under the heading's row. 6vw of 800 is 48,
  between 32 and 80, so the gap is 48 both ways; the 712 left of the
  section's 760 is shared .8 to 1.2, 284.8 and 427.2.
  `tests/alo_sites_contact_form.rs` says so.

Everything else checks against the sheet by hand and is pinned as it is:
`main > section` is 70rem at most, so on this page 800 wide with 3rem and
1.25rem of padding, its content 760 at (20, 48). The heading is 28 px
(`clamp(1.5rem, 3.5vw, 2.125rem)`) at a line height of 1.2, and "Wholesale
enquiries" is wider than 284.8 at that size, so it is two lines, 67.2, with
0.5em (14) under it: its row is 81.2. The introduction, 17 px at 1.6, is two
lines, 54.4, at 48 + 81.2 + 48 = 177.2. The form is placed in rows one to
three, `align-items: start`, and is 442.5 tall; what it needs beyond the
first two rows goes to the third, which holds nothing else, as CSS Grid 1
§ 11.5 distributes a spanning item's size only up to each track's limit, and
the first two are already at theirs. So the section is 48 + 442.5 + 48 =
538.5. In the form: each label is a 27.2 block with 0.25rem under it; each
field is `width: 100%`, 427.2, with 0.6rem and 0.75rem of padding and a 1 px
border, so 27.2 + 19.2 + 2 = 48.4 tall; the message field is
`min-height: 8rem`, 128, and as an inline block standing on its bottom edge
it leaves the strut's descent, 7.7, under it in its line, as browsers do;
each paragraph has 1em (17) under it. The button is 27.2 + 19.2 + 2 = 48.4
tall and its text plus 1.2rem each side and its border, 88.4, wide. The
honeypot paragraph is `position: absolute !important`, 1 × 1, clipped and
hidden from the agent tree by `aria-hidden`; its `overflow: hidden` keeps
its label and field inside that one pixel, which none of their ink reaches,
so nothing of it is drawn (checked in pixels: white at (352..353, 48..49)).
Its `clip-path: inset(50%)` is not read by this engine, and an unknown
property is kept and ignored without a record, so issues.txt does not name
it; here it would hide nothing more. The
skip link is `left: -999rem`, as on the other cases.

What the sheet asks for that this engine does not do is in issues.txt: the

`@keyframes`, and `prefers-reduced-motion`. Nothing on this page uses any of
