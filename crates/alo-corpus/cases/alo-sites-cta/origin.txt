Why this page. It opened queue item 347: like every page alo Sites
publishes, it links its whole style sheet and carries an inline script, so a
renderer loads it, and until item 348 nothing handed a renderer a linked
sheet. ADR 0035 decided how one is asked for; item 348 built that, and item
349 froze this. It is the smallest alo Sites page — one section — and the
only one whose every request can be frozen with provenance: the hero has an
`<img>` whose bytes are in the site's store and in no repository (item 350).

**Where it is served from: `https://nordwind.alosites.com/`** (address.txt).
The page's own `<link rel="canonical">` and `og:url` say so, and alo Sites'
tests render the demonstration site with that `base_url`.

**What its sheet is answered with: site.css** (linked.txt). The page links
`/assets/site.css`, which resolves against the address to

- https://nordwind.alosites.com/assets/site.css

and alo Sites' `src/serve.rs` serves exactly that path with
`Content-Type: text/css; charset=utf-8`. The corpus types a frozen file by
its extension, as `alo-net` types a `file:` URL, and `.css` is that same
type; so the sheet passes the browser process's check that only a 2xx
`text/css` answer crosses (ADR 0035 § 3), here as live. The renderer asks
for it in its load's answer, the corpus answers it from this file, and the
reference is the page drawn after that answer, as the window would show it
once ADR 0035 § 5 (item 351) holds back the first frame. Its first draw, at
load, said truly that no sheet had arrived yet.

**What its script does offline: nothing it could send.** The script is alo
Sites' own analytics. It reads the time, the path and the window's width,
and posts what a visitor reads, clicks and scrolls with
`navigator.sendBeacon("/_alo/collect", …)` — only when a visitor leaves or
hides the page, clicks, or meets a form whose `action` starts `/f/`, and
only if `navigator.sendBeacon` exists. This page has no such form, and a
reference render clicks nothing and never leaves, so even a script that ran
to its end would ask for nothing here. It does not run to its end: it stops
at its third line, `var since = Date.now();`, with "ReferenceError: 'Date'
is not defined" (queue item 353), before it adds a single listener. Nothing
it would have done changes the picture.

Rendered at 800 × 600: wider than the sheet's one breakpoint here,
`max-width: 42rem` (672 px), and wide enough that `h2`'s
`clamp(1.5rem, 3.5vw, 2.125rem)` is its middle term, 28 px.

## What it found

- **The sheet arrives and is applied** — items 348 and 349. The section is
  a band of the theme's `--primary` (#1d4ed8), 0.75rem rounded, its text
  centred in white; its two buttons sit side by side `0.75rem` apart,
  centred: the first white with blue text, the second clear with a white
  border and white text. Every number in layout.txt checks against the
  sheet by hand, and `tests/alo_sites_cta.rs` says so.
- **The skip link is out of flow** — item 352. `.skip-link` is
  `position: absolute; left: -999rem; top: 0`, which takes it out of flow
  and off the page until it is focused. Until item 352 the inline `<a>`
  stayed in an anonymous line at the top left, drawn on its `--surface`
  grey, and the section started 27.2 px down (one line of `body`). Now it
  is blockified and laid out at (-15984, 0), and the section starts at 0.
  The agent still reads the link, where it is, because a person can tab
  to it.
- **The script stops at `Date`** — item 353, as above.

What the sheet asks for that this engine does not do, and which makes no
difference to this page, is in issues.txt: `::before` and `::after`
selectors, `@keyframes`, and the `prefers-reduced-motion` media feature,
which is treated as not matching — the answer for a person who has not
asked for reduced motion, which is the corpus's. `system-ui` comes first in
the font list and is drawn in DejaVu Sans, as every case is.

The reference render pins the page as it renders today, faults included, so
that each of those items moves it and says so.
