alo-workplace, web/src/meet/MeetModule.tsx — the greeting on alo's Meet screen.

Written out 2026-10-07 from the `alo-workplace` repository checked out beside
this one, at its commit 738de61401d992c9b058c83237e16c90ecbd23ef. React
renders the screen, so page.html is the markup it produces for the header
(lines 153–157 of MeetModule.tsx) inside the screen's `.module` and
`.content`, with the English strings of web/src/i18n/en.ts: the morning
greeting, no first name, "Your meeting space" and the subtitle. style.css is
web/src/meet/MeetModule.module.css's rules for those elements,
web/src/ds/global.css's for `html` and `body`, and the tokens they read from
web/src/ds/tokens.css. `alo-workplace` is read-only reference; nothing was
written back to it.

The picture is `wavingHand`, web/src/assets/alo-waving-hand.svg, frozen byte
for byte once, in ../../pictures/alo-waving-hand/ (its origin.txt has the
hash), and named by linked.txt under the `/assets/` path the build serves it
from. A frozen file had no response, so its `.svg` stands in for the type
`image/svg+xml` (ADR 0027 § 1).

Why this page. ADR 0027 was opened by it, and queue item 310 is closed by it:
the greeting draws `<img class="greetingIcon" src={wavingHand} alt="">`, an
SVG file shown as a picture. Before item 310 that was a 20 × 20 hole.

Rendered at 800 × 220, wider than the screen's 48rem breakpoint.

## What it found

**The hand is drawn**, which is what the page was frozen for: the file's
two paths, terracotta rgb(231 111 81) and navy rgb(16 42 67), each `evenodd`,
met into the <img>'s 20 × 20 content box from the file's 395 × 385 viewBox
and clipped there (display.txt). `tests/meets_greeting.rs` says it in
numbers. The agent reads one decorative image with nothing inside it,
because `alt=""` (agent.txt).

**Three faults around it**, none of them the picture's, each queued rather
than fixed in the iteration that found them:

- `vertical-align` was read by nothing, so `middle` left the hand on the
  line's baseline, a few pixels above where browsers put it — item 312.
  **Fixed by item 312 (iteration 193):** the hand's middle is now at the
  greeting's baseline plus half the x-height of its own face, DejaVu Sans
  Bold's `x`. The hand is the tallest thing on the line, so the line is its
  20 pixels and the text moved up into it; everything below moved up by the
  same 3.07.
- `.module`'s background is two layers, a radial-gradient tint and a
  colour, and a background of more than one layer draws nothing. The tint
  in the top right corner is missing, and nothing records it — item 313.
  **Fixed by item 313 (iteration 194):** both layers are drawn, the page's
  colour beneath and the tint over it, a circle centred at 92% across the
  top whose stops reach transparent 26% of the way to the bottom left
  corner. `tests/meets_greeting.rs` reads its pixels back.
- `(max-width: 48rem)` is recorded as not understood, because a media
  query's length cannot be in `rem` here. At this width it would not match
  anyway, so nothing visible changes; on a narrow window it would — item 315.
  **Fixed by item 315 (iteration 195):** `48rem` is 768 px, from the
  initial 16 px font size, and the issue is gone. The render is unchanged
  at this width; `tests/meets_greeting.rs` lays the page out at 768 and
  769 and finds the narrow padding and heading only at the first.
- `line-height` set the height of no line, so the heading at
  `--leading-tight` was its face's 37.25 rather than 40, the last line at
  `--leading-normal` its face's 17.46 rather than 22.5, and everything under
  the heading sat high — item 316, found by item 315's test.
  **Fixed by item 316 (iteration 196):** each line is as tall as its
  `line-height`s, with half of the leading above the text and half below.
  The heading is 40 and its text 1.375 down in it, the last paragraph 22.5,
  and the greeting's line 20.70: the 13 px strut at 19.5 reaches higher
  above the baseline than the middle-aligned hand, which still reaches
  lower below it. The banner grew from 86.71 to 95.20.

The issues list also has the two `::before` and `::after` selectors of
global.css's `box-sizing` rule, which stage 1 does not produce.

The reference render pins the page as it renders today, faults included, so
that each of those items moves it and says so.
