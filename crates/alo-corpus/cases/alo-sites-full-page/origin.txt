## What it found

- **An `inline-flex` was broken around the block in it** — item 286, built.
  The brand is `<a class="brand"><img class="logo" alt="Nordwind Coffee
  Roasters"></a>`; the nav makes every link `inline-flex` and the sheet
  makes every `<img>` `display: block`. The box tree broke the link around
  the picture as CSS breaks a `<span>` around a block: two empty links 0
  wide at x 20 and 403.29, and the picture between them, a block of its own
  at x 93.63. A flex container's children are its items whatever their
  `display`, and an `inline-flex` is one atomic box on its line. Now the
  link is one box at (20, 14.2), 44 tall, holding the picture at (20, 20.2),
  32 tall — Chrome's numbers exactly. Its width is its `alt`'s, 293.65;
  Chrome's is 309.66, the 16 of the broken-picture icon it draws before the
  text (item 418). `tests/alo_sites_full_page.rs` says so.
- **A closed `<details>` showed what it hides** — item 429, built by ADR
  0048. The FAQ's one `<details>` has no `open`, and Chrome shows only its
  summary, 53.19 tall; here it was 92.4 tall, its answer drawn, and an
  agent read the answer while also reading `expanded=false` on the group.
  Now it is 53.2, its answer neither laid out, drawn nor read, and every
  section below it stands 39.2 higher than it did; the summary reads
  `[expanded=false]`, and activating it opens the `details` to 92.4, as
  Chrome's. `tests/alo_sites_full_page.rs` says so. Still different: no
  disclosure triangle is drawn (item 432), so the question starts 16.94
  left of Chrome's, and every section below the FAQ stands 12 lower than
  Chrome's because Chrome's columns (below) drop the `<details>`' 0.75rem
  margin at a column's end.
- **A border 4.8 wide was drawn 4.8 wide** — item 430, built. The
  custom-code section's `border-left: .3rem` is 4.8 px, and Chrome draws
  and lays out 4: a border width of a device pixel or more is floored to
  whole device pixels. Now it is 4 here too, and the section's content
  starts at 44, as in Chrome; it was 44.8. `tests/alo_sites_full_page.rs`
  says so.

Everything else matches Chrome to its layout unit apart from what is already
in the queue: the FAQ is `columns: 2 20rem` (item 97, multi-column, needs
design), so its `<details>` is 760 wide where Chrome's is 372; the nav is
`position: sticky` (item 97 again, recorded in issues.txt); the custom code
is an `<iframe>` (item 86, needs design), a box of its size with nothing in
it; and the sections set to move are drawn at opacity 0 and 24 down until
`IntersectionObserver` exists (item 381), which is also why Chrome, running
the page's motion script, puts the custom-code section 40 lower and the
footer 40 higher than here, the two together the same. The pricing tier's
`translateY(-.75rem)` moves Chrome's reported box 12 up; it is drawn so here
(display.txt) and does not move layout in either.

What the sheet asks for that this engine does not do is in issues.txt: the

`@keyframes`, and `prefers-reduced-motion` — the nav's `position: sticky`,
and the pictures nobody has.
