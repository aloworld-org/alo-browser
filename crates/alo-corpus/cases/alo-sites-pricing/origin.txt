## What it found

- **`color-mix()` was refused, and the shadow with it** — item 377. The
  engine read no colour space but sRGB's own functions and refused
  `color-mix()` whole, and a shadow whose colour cannot be read is refused
  whole, as CSS refuses a declaration it cannot parse. So the highlighted
  card was drawn lifted and flat. A mix *in sRGB* converts nothing, so it is
  now read: `#17212b` at 12% over nothing is `#17212b` at an alpha of 0.12,
  and the card casts it, 1.5rem down and blurred over 3rem, under the same
  transform as the card. `tests/alo_sites_pricing.rs` says so in numbers.

Everything else checks against the sheet by hand and is pinned as it is:
`repeat(auto-fit, minmax(16rem, 1fr))` in 760 px collapses its empty second
track and gives the one tier the whole width; the tier's layout box stays
at y 139.8 and is drawn 12 px higher; `.price` is 28 px with its
`.period` at 16 beside it; each feature is a line of `body` and 0.35rem
above and below, over a 1 px rule.

What the sheet asks for that this engine does not do, and which makes no
difference to this page, is in issues.txt, and is the call-to-action
section's list exactly: `::before` and `::after`, `@keyframes`, and
`prefers-reduced-motion`.
