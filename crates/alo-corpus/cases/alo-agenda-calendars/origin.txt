alo-workplace, web/src/agenda/AgendaModule.tsx — the calendar list in the
sidebar of alo's Agenda (calendar) screen.

Written out 2026-10-11 from the `alo-workplace` repository checked out beside
this one, at its commit 738de61401d992c9b058c83237e16c90ecbd23ef. React
renders the screen, so page.html is the markup it produces for the sidebar
(AgendaModule.tsx, lines 283–381: `calendarRow` and the `<aside>` around it),
in English (web/src/i18n/en.ts). The calendars are a fixture of four, in the
shape the screen draws: two the viewer owns — "Personal", the personal
calendar (no delete button), and "Work" — and two shared with them — "Team
holidays", which they can edit and have hidden, and "Mira's calendar, shared
from the design team", which they can view and whose name is longer than its
row. Each one's colour is the one `calendarColorMap` (web/src/agenda/colors.ts)
assigns by order, written as React writes `style={{"--cal": colour}}`. The
icons are lucide-react 0.468.0's `CalendarPlus`, `Plus`, `Check`, `Share2`,
`Trash2` and `Clock` at the sizes the screen asks for, written out as the
`<svg>` that library renders (its `defaultAttributes.js` and `Icon.js`, and
each icon's own shapes).
style.css is the rules of web/src/agenda/AgendaModule.module.css those
elements match, web/src/ds/global.css's reset, `body`, `button` and `svg`
rules, and the tokens they read from web/src/ds/tokens.css. `:hover` rules
and `body`'s font-smoothing hints are left out; nothing else is changed.
`alo-workplace` is read-only reference; nothing was written back to it.

The month navigator between the "New event" button and the lists is its own
case, `alo-agenda-mini-month`, and is not here.

Rendered at 280 × 340.

Why this page. The workspace's own screens are where the next real pages come
from (iteration 274), and this is the list a person ticks every day to choose
which calendars they see: rows that grow by `flex: 1`, a name cut short by
`text-overflow`, headings and badges set by `text-transform`, and a colour
handed to each row through a custom property in its `style` attribute.

## What it found

Measured against headless Chrome 154 on the same bytes, given the corpus's
DejaVu Sans through `@font-face`:

- Each row's toggle grows by `flex: 1`, and nothing split or read the `flex`
  shorthand, so each toggle was as wide as its name (79.26 for "Personal",
  where Chrome's is 178) and the share and delete buttons stood against it
  rather than at the row's end — item 440. alo Settings, already in the
  corpus, had the same fault unseen.
  **Fixed by item 440 (iteration 281):** `flex` is split into its three
  longhands as it is written. The toggles are 178 and 157 wide, the
  buttons at 206, 185 and 206, every box but the two below Chrome's.
- The two list headings are `text-transform: uppercase` and the two access
  badges `lowercase`; this engine draws the text as written. "My calendars"
  is 95.46 wide where Chrome's "MY CALENDARS" is 111.69, and "Can edit"
  50.19 where "can edit" is 48.42, which moves the toggle beside it by
  1.77 — item 441, which needs an ADR for what an agent reads.
- Mira's calendar's name ends in "…" in Chrome (`text-overflow:
  ellipsis`) and is cut mid-letter at its box's edge here — item 442.

`tests/alo_agenda_calendars.rs` says it in numbers.

What the agent reads is good: each calendar is a button named by the
calendar, with `pressed` saying whether it is shown, and the share and
delete buttons are named, though they are drawn at opacity 0 until the row
is under a pointer.

The issues list has only the two `::before` and `::after` selectors of
global.css's `box-sizing` rule.
