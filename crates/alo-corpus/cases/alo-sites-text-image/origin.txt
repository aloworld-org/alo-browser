## What it found

- **The text card ignored its `margin-inline-start: -4rem`** — item 380.
  This engine mapped no inline-axis logical property, so the card stood in
  its own column, 364 wide at x 416, its padding leaving 300 for text and
  its sentence on two lines; the section was 279 tall. The card now starts
  at 416 − 64 = 352 and, a stretched grid item, takes the margin into its
  width: 364 + 64 = 428, over the right end of the picture's column. Its
  margin is left to right's: a right-to-left page would want it on the
  other side, and that is item 421. `tests/alo_sites_text_image.rs` says so.

Everything else checks against the sheet by hand and is pinned as it is.

padding at the sides and 3rem above and below: (800 − 40 − 32) / 2 = 364
