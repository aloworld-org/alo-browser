## What it found

- **`padding-block` was not read** — item 379. The sheet gives a bento
  grid's first card `padding-block: 2.5rem`, and the engine split no
  logical shorthand, so the card kept the 1.25rem all round that
  `.section-cards` gives every card: 116.8 px tall where it should be 156.8.
  `padding-block` and `margin-block` are now split into the top and bottom
  they are in the one writing mode this engine lays out, and the card is
  40 px inside its border above and below. `tests/alo_sites_features.rs`
  says so in numbers, and `cases/block-axis-spacing` shows the same rule as
  a picture.
- **The whole section is drawn at opacity 0, so the reference render is
  white** — item 381. The page's first script adds `js` to `<html>`, which
  makes every `.section-motion` transparent and 1.5rem low until a class
  `is-visible` is added; and only an `IntersectionObserver` adds it. This
  engine has none, and the script asks `"IntersectionObserver" in window`
  before using it, so nothing fails and nothing is shown. That is what this
  page does in any browser without the interface — the page's choice, drawn
  correctly — and it is why the picture here is blank while the layout,
  the display list and the agent's tree are not. The sheet's own escape,
  `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`, is not understood and so does
  not match, which is what a person who has not asked for less motion gets
  anyway.

Everything else checks against the sheet by hand and is pinned as it is:
`main > section`'s 1.25rem sides and `section-spacing-generous`'s 5rem top
and bottom put the heading at (20, 80); `h2`'s `clamp(1.5rem, 3.5vw,
2.125rem)` is 28 px at 800 wide; the intro is 45rem wide and its text is
centred in it; the grid is two `minmax(0, 1fr)` tracks and the first card
spans `1 / -1`, so it is the whole 760; the card's background is
`color-mix(in srgb, #4c5866 92%, #17212b)`, `rgb(72 84 97)`; and the
section is drawn through `translateY(1.5rem)`, 24 px.

What the sheet asks for that this engine does not do, and which makes no
difference to this page beyond the above, is in issues.txt, and is the
call-to-action section's list exactly: `::before` and `::after`,
`@keyframes`, and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
