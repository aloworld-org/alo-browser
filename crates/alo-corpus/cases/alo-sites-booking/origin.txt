## What it found

- **The empty day field stood a font's descent too high** — item 384. The
  field has nothing typed in it, so it held no line, and an atomic box with
  no line stands on its bottom margin edge (item 285). Browsers stand a
  one-line field on the baseline of the line it would hold, typed into or
  not, so it does not drop when somebody types. Here the field's line was
  56.11 rather than its own 48.4 (the strut's 7.71 below the baseline at
  17 px and 1.6), which made the form 7.71 taller and, centred, moved it and
  its label up by half that. `tests/alo_sites_booking.rs` says so in numbers.
  `web-a-form`'s three empty fields had the same fault, and moved with it.

- **Not built: what an empty date field shows** — item 385. A browser draws
  the date's format in it (`mm/dd/yyyy` in English) and a button that opens
  a calendar. This engine draws the field empty, as it draws any field with
  no value. Which format is the person's locale, which is theirs to tell, so
  it waits for a design rather than being guessed here.
