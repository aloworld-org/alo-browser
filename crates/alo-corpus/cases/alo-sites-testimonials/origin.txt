## What it found

- **The quote was drawn upright** — item 382. `.testimonial blockquote`
  says `font-style: italic`, and the corpus's fonts (DejaVu Sans regular and
  bold, DejaVu Serif) have no slanted face, so the face chosen is the
  upright one. A browser given the same fonts leans the upright face, since
  `font-synthesis`' initial value allows a synthesised style (CSS Fonts 4
  § 3.3); this engine drew it as it was, and nothing said so. The quote is
  now drawn leaned by 14°, CSS's `oblique` with no angle, about its
  baseline, with its advances and so its layout unchanged.
  `tests/alo_sites_testimonials.rs` says so in the display list and in
  pixels.

Everything else checks against the sheet by hand and is pinned as it is:
`main > section`'s 3rem top and 1.25rem sides put the heading at (20, 48);
`h2`'s `clamp(1.5rem, 3.5vw, 2.125rem)` is 28 px at 800 wide, 33.6 tall at
a line height of 1.2, with 14 px below; the list is two `minmax(0, 1fr)`
tracks and the first item spans `1 / -1`, so the card is the whole 760; the
card's 1 px border and 48 px padding put the quote at (69, 144.6), 662
wide; the quote is 24 px (3vw) at a line height of 1.3, 31.2 tall; the
paragraph's 1em below (24 px) collapses through the blockquote with its
1rem, so the caption is at 199.8, 27.2 tall at the body's 17 px and 1.6;
and the card is 180.4 tall and the section 324.

What the sheet asks for that this engine does not do, and which makes no
difference to this page, is in issues.txt, and is the call-to-action
