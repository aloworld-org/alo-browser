## What it found

- **A box out of flow broke the line it was in** — item 354, built. Every
  card ends in `<a class="blog-read">Read article<span class="sr-only">:
  Launch notes</span></a>`, and `.sr-only` is `position: absolute`, one
  pixel square with `margin: -1px`. The engine broke the link around the
  `<span>` as around a block: the link was two pieces, the `<span>` a block
  of its own at x 44 below the link's line, and an agent read the link "in
  2 pieces" at (44, 768.08). Now the line is whole and the `<span>` stands
  at its static position: the end of "Read article" less its margin,
  45 + 115.42236 − 1 = 159.42236 across, and the top of the link's line
  less its margin, 764.375 − 1 = 763.375 down. Chrome puts it at the same
  place relative to the link: 45 + 115.4375 − 1 = 159.4375, one pixel above
  the line's top. `tests/alo_sites_blog_index.rs` says so.
- **The cards are one column, and Chrome's are two** — item 422, not built.
  `.blog-grid` is `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(100%, 18rem), 1fr))`. `taffy`
  counts an `auto-fit` repetition only when every track's size is a plain
  length or percentage; a `min()` is a calculation, so it throws the whole
  template away and every card is a row of its own, 760 wide, where Chrome
  puts two of 368 side by side. Working around a rented crate's refusal is
  a decision rather than a chore, so it waits for its ADR. This is pinned as
  it is: the cover 758 × 426.375 (16:9 of 758) and the list 800.575 tall.

Everything else checks against the sheet by hand and is pinned as it is. The
first card's copy is 1.5rem of padding each side and, in one column of 758
less its 1px border, 710 wide: the date, 15 px at 1.6, 24 with 15 below it;
the heading, 1.5rem at 1.2, 28.8 with 0.5rem below; the summary, one line of
27.2 with 17 below; and the link's line, 27.2: 24 + 39 + 36.8 + 44.2 + 27.2
+ 24 = 195.2. The pager is a grid of `1fr auto 1fr`: "Page 2 of 3" in the
middle, its links pushed to each end by `justify-self`.

What the sheet asks for that this engine does not do is in issues.txt: the
