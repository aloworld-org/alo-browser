## What it found

- **A paragraph's narrowest was its whole line** — item 394. The split
  layout is `grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr`, and a `1fr` column is never
  narrower than what is in it can be. Layout asked text how narrow it could
  be by measuring it with no width, which is the max-content answer, so the
  notice "This is not taking bookings at the moment." claimed its whole
  365.6 as a minimum: the columns came out 320.4 and 365.6. The narrowest
  is the widest piece that cannot be broken (CSS Sizing 3 § 5.1); now the
  columns are 343 each and the notice wraps after "the", as a browser
  draws it. `tests/alo_sites_booking_closed.rs` says so.

Everything else checks against the sheet by hand and is pinned as it is:
`.s-booking`'s 1.5rem of padding wins over `main > section`'s 3rem by
specificity, so with its 1 px border the content is at (25, 25) and 750
wide; the gap is 1rem by 4rem; with no `.booking-day` the six blocks are
placed two to a row, the heading beside the consultation's name, which is
what the sheet asks for when there is no field to put in the second
column. The rows are 47.6 (28 px heading at 1.2 and its 14 below), 44.2
and 71.4, the shorter block in each stretched to its row, and the section
is 245.2 tall.

What the sheet asks for that this engine does not do, and which makes no
difference to this page, is in issues.txt, and is the call-to-action
section's list exactly: `::before` and `::after`, `@keyframes`, and
`prefers-reduced-motion`.
