alo browser

This page is drawn by the Chromium alo browser carries, driven by alo browser.

What this is

A browser an agent reads rather than photographs. An agent asks alo browser for this page as a tree of what it is — a heading, a paragraph, a link — and acts on it by name, under the sites a person lets it reach. Every request the page makes is held to alo browser's rules and kept in its record.

The frame above this window says which renderer draws the page, and what each agent asked for and was answered.

What it is not yet

There is no address bar, so a person cannot go anywhere from here. An agent opens other pages through alo-ask, which is inside the application at Contents/MacOS/alo-ask.

Chromium may ask Google for two things of its own that are not held to alo browser's rules or kept in its record: which Google accounts it is signed into, and the list of its on-device models. alo browser starts it with everything else it would ask for of its own switched off, and never uses another browser on this Mac, even one that is installed.

A page at a name ending in .local, such as this Mac's own, waits until macOS lets alo browser's Chromium look on the local network. macOS asks whether to allow its helper to find devices on local networks; choose Allow, or allow it later in System Settings, under Privacy & Security, Local Network.

Closing this window, or quitting, closes alo browser.
