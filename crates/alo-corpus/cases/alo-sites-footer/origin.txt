## What it found

- **The skip link was placed against `body` rather than the page** — item
  355. `.skip-link` is `position: absolute; top: 0; left: -999rem`, and
  nothing above it is positioned, so CSS 2 § 10.1 places it against the
  initial containing block: the top of the page. Layout placed every
  absolute box against its parent. On every other alo Sites case `body` is
  at the top of the page, so that was right by coincidence; here `main` is
  empty and the footer's `margin-top: 3rem` collapses through it and through
  `body`, so `body` starts 48 down, and the link was drawn at y 48. It is
  off the page to the left either way, so no pixel showed it — the layout
  tree, which an agent reads, did. `tests/alo_sites_footer.rs` says so.

Everything else checks against the sheet by hand and is pinned as it is:
`body` and the footer are both at y 48, by that collapsed margin, and the
document is 48 + 124.2 = 172.2 tall. The footer is a grid, `1fr 1fr` with
`gap: 1rem 3rem`, `align-items: center`; `section-spacing-standard` makes
its padding 3rem above and below, `.s-footer`'s 1.25rem stays at the sides,
and its border-top is 1 px, so its content is 760 wide at (20, 97), and each
column is (760 − 48) / 2 = 356. The navigation and the copyright line are
`width: 100%` of their columns, at x 20 and 20 + 356 + 48 = 424, and the
line is `text-align: right`: its text ends at 780. Both are one line of
17 px at 1.6, 27.2, so the footer is 1 + 48 + 27.2 + 48 = 124.2. The list is
a flex row with `gap: 1rem`: "Imprint" is 61.5 wide and "Privacy" starts 16
after it. The footer's own `max-width: none` and its children's
`max-width: 90rem` (`section-width-wide`) take nothing off at 800.
