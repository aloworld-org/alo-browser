Why this page. Every alo Sites golden was frozen or measured (iteration

person and an agent both read every day.

## What it found

Measured against headless Chrome 154 on the same bytes, given the corpus's
DejaVu Sans through `@font-face`, every element's box matched to Chrome's
1/64 px layout unit, and the two screenshots agree, **except one thing**:

- Each arrow is a `<button>` with 2 px of padding holding a 15 px `<svg>`
  that global.css makes `display: block`. Chrome draws it 19 × 19. Here the
  box a control holds its content in was at least a line tall whatever it
  held — 19.5 at 13 px and 1.5 — so each arrow was 23.5 tall, the header row
  grew from 19.5 to 23.5, and the grid stood 4 px low — item 437.
  **Fixed by item 437 (iteration 280):** a `<button>` element is at least a
  line tall only when it holds something a line is made of. Every other
  control (a field, a `<textarea>`, `<input type=submit value="">`) is a
  line tall even when empty, as Chrome draws them. The arrows are 19 × 19 at
  (183, 16.25) and (204, 16.25), the grid starts at 43.5, and the last week
  at 209.95 (Chrome: 209.97). No other case in the corpus moved.

`tests/alo_agenda_mini_month.rs` says it in numbers.

What the agent reads is alo's markup, and it is thin: 42 buttons named by
the day's number, with nothing saying which month a grey one is in, which is
today, or which is picked. The page says none of that — no `aria-current`,
no `aria-pressed`, no full date in a name — so an engine that invented it
would be guessing from colour, which ADR 0002 forbids. It is a finding for
`alo-workplace`, which this repository does not edit.

The issues list has only the two `::before` and `::after` selectors of
global.css's `box-sizing` rule.
