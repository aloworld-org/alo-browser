## What it found

- **`max-width: none` was recorded as a value not implemented** — item
  393. `.s-tickets` sets `max-width: 38rem` and `.tickets-layout-banner`
  lifts it with `none`. Layout read a maximum as it reads a width, which
  has no `none`, so it refused the value and fell back to the initial
  value, which is `none`: the box was right, and issues.txt said
  "value not implemented, property left at its initial: max-width: none"
  against a page that had written nothing wrong. It reads `none` now, and
  issues.txt is the other alo Sites cases' list exactly.
  `tests/alo_sites_tickets.rs` says so.

Everything else checks against the sheet by hand and is pinned as it is:
the banner's `clamp(2rem, 5vw, 4rem)` is 40 at 800 wide, inside a 1 px
border, so the content is at (41, 41), 718 wide; the heading is 28 px
(3.5vw) at a line height of 1.2, 33.6 tall, with 14 below, so the offer is
at 88.6; its 27.2 and its 17 px margin, collapsed with the actions' 1rem,
put the actions at 132.8; the button is 48.4 tall, and the actions' 17
below and 40 + 1 make the section 239.2. `text-align: center` centres the
heading and the offer; the actions are a flex row with no
`justify-content`, so the button stands at the start, at 41, which is
where a browser draws it too.

What the sheet asks for that this engine does not do, and which makes no
difference to this page, is in issues.txt, and is the call-to-action
section's list exactly: `::before` and `::after`, `@keyframes`, and
`prefers-reduced-motion`.
