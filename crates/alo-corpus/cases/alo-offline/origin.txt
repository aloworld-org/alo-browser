Why this page. It is the page ADR 0022 named as the one that closes queue item
273: its only picture is an inline <svg> of the alo hand — four <path>s with
arcs, stroked in terracotta at two units of a 24-unit viewBox, fill="none",
round caps and round joins — drawn into a 56-pixel square. Before item 273 that
square was empty. The page is ours, so freezing it needs nobody's leave, and
anybody can re-derive it from the commit above.

It is fully self-contained on purpose (its own comment says why: offline is
exactly when a second request would fail), so the case needs no linked.txt. It
carries a script, so the corpus loads it through a renderer and the script
runs; the script picks a language from `navigator.language` and, finding
English, leaves the markup's English as it is. The issues list is empty.

Rendered at 480 × 400, about a phone held sideways.

## What it found

**The hand is drawn**, which is what the page was frozen for: four outlines
in rgb(231 111 81) inside the <svg>'s 56 × 56 content box, each two units ×
56/24 = 4⅔ pixels wide with round ends and corners (display.txt). Its geometry
is pinned in numbers by `alo-svg`'s walk test
`the_offline_screens_hand_is_four_terracotta_strokes_inside_its_box`.

**Three layout faults around it**, none of them the hand's, each queued rather
than fixed in the iteration that found them:

- `text-align: center` does not move an atomic inline, so the <svg> and the
  <button> sit at the left of `main` where browsers centre them — item 280.
- The <svg>'s `margin-bottom: 20px` is not in its line's height, so the
  heading starts straight under the hand — item 281.
- `place-items: center` is a shorthand nothing expands, so `main` stretches to
  the grid's height instead of being centred in it — item 282.

The reference render pins the page as it renders today, faults included, so
that each of those items moves it and says so.
